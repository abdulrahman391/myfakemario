A browser platformer · 5 worlds · 50 levels

Run, jump and light the beacons.

Sprocket is a small tin robot who lost his way home. Stomp beetles, dodge spikers and bats, open crates for six power-ups and reach the radio beacon at the end of every level before the clock runs out.

How to play

Controls

RunHold Shift or X
Start / pauseEnter or P
SoundM
GamepadD-pad to move, A to jump, X or B to run, Y to fire

On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen buttons under the game.

  1. Hold jump to jump higher. Tap it for a short hop.
  2. Land on enemies to stomp them. Stomp several without touching the ground for bigger points.
  3. Bump crates from below. Gear crates give a gear; some hold a battery.
  4. A battery charges Sprocket. While charged he can smash bricks and survive one hit.
  5. Touch a lamp post to save your progress in the middle of a level.
  6. Every 50 gears earns an extra life. Leftover time turns into points.
  7. Crates with a picture hold power-ups: Spark, Boots, Star, Magnet and Clock. See the list below.
  8. Finish a world to unlock the next one and earn a life. On the title screen use left and right to pick a world you have unlocked.

About this game

Sprocket's Run is an original game inspired by classic side-scrolling platformers. Every character, level, sprite, sound and tune was made for this project. The 50 levels are built from hand-designed pieces and a fixed seed, so each one is always the same. It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so it runs offline straight from the folder and needs no install or build step.

Your best score is saved in this browser. Want to make your own levels? Open js/levels.js: the README explains the tile legend.
